# 📄 README

This repository contains the source code for my Obsidian Vault, published via Quartz 5.0. It follows an organized PARA + Zettelkasten hybrid system to ensure high data availability and low mental friction.

For more info about my notes management, check this out: 👉 [PARA](2025-15-10-Para-Method) and [Zettelkasten](2026-07-27-Zettelkasten-Method).


---

### 📂 Folder Structure (The Blueprint)

My vault is organized into five primary directories to keep things actionable:

- **00 Projects/** — Active missions with a deadline.
    
- **01 Areas/** — Ongoing responsibilities (e.g., Backend Architecture).
    
- **02 Resources/** — Knowledge inputs, references, and topics of interest for future exploration.
    
- **03 Permanent/** — My Zettelkasten. Long-term, atomic, and interconnected thoughts written entirely in my own words.
    
- **04 Archives/** — Completed quests and past projects.


---

### 📊 Database Logic (Bases)

To handle high-volume data like my book list and course tracking, I use Bases.

- **Areas-base:** Tracks ongoing goals and important areas of my life.
    
- **Resources-base:** My central Inbox for books, courses, projects, apps, and useful URLs I am currently exploring.
    
- **Permanent-base:** Indexes the atomic notes I've created, structured in a way that makes the most sense for my learning process.
    

> **Note:** These Bases are highly interactive within Obsidian, but are rendered as static tables or links here in the Quartz web view.

### 💬 Feedback & Reactions

I highly value your input! **You can add comments and react directly to my notes.**

Because this vault is built on the philosophy of learning in public, your feedback is incredibly important to me. Whether you are pointing out a technical correction, sharing a different perspective on backend design, or just leaving a reaction to show you found a note helpful, it keeps me motivated. It helps me refine my understanding and makes this digital garden a collaborative space rather than just a one-way broadcast.
